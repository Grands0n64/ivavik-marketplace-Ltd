# Ivavik Marketplace Ltd - Business Overview

Ivavik Marketplace Ltd is a premium, multi-vendor e-commerce platform designed to bridge the gap between retail vendors and consumers across Africa. Just like Jumia, Ivavik allows independent businesses to create storefronts, upload product images, and sell directly to customers, while providing a secure shopping experience.

## Our Vision
To become a leading digital marketplace known for fast delivery, verified vendors, and reliable payment systems.

## Core Features of the Platform
* Multi-Vendor Dashboards: Separate login portals for sellers to manage inventory.
* Secure Payment Gateway: Integrated checkout supporting cards, bank transfers, and mobile money.
* Dynamic Product Catalog: Capacity to scale and support over 500+ diverse products across electronics, fashion, computing, and beauty.
* Mobile-First Layout: Optimized configuration specifically engineered for smooth browsing on mobile networks and phones.
